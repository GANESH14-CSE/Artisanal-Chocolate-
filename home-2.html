SPIRIT PAIRING #1

Islay Peated Single Malt × 74% Smoked Sea Salt Dark Truffle

The bold smoky peat and maritime brine of an aged Islay whiskey cuts cleanly through the unctuous single-estate cocoa butter, allowing hidden notes of toasted macadamia and dried apricots to bloom on the palate.

Sommelier Tasting Ritual:

Take a small sip of whiskey, allow it to coat the tongue, place a bite of the truffle on the palate, and let the cocoa butter melt naturally without chewing.
