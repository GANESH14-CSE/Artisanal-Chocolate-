A JOURNEY OF DEVOTION

Our Story

Meridian Cacao began not in a boardroom, but in a sunlit kitchen around a solitary 5kg stone melanger. We were driven by a singular, intoxicating revelation: commercial chocolate had forgotten what cocoa actually tasted like.

Real cocoa is fruit. It is alive with wild raspberries, mountain blossoms, forest honey, and rich earthy soils. We set out to honor that truth. Our very first batch of Madagascar 70% single-estate dark chocolate sold out within two hours at a local farmers' market.

As our reputation quietly spread among culinary critics, pastry chefs, and discerning chocolate lovers, we remained steadfast in our founding creed: slow stone conching, zero palm oil or fillers, 100% pure cocoa butter, and deep personal partnerships with the regenerative farmers who cultivate heirloom cacao.

LOOKING FORWARD

Our Vision

Our dream is to expand the global palate for authentic, uncompromised chocolate while proving that luxury and ecological regenerative agriculture can thrive together.

We are actively investing in cacao seed sanctuaries in Latin America and Africa to save endangered ancient wild Criollo varieties from commercial extinction. Every bar you savor with us directly finances tree plantings, fair living wages, and educational bursaries for farming families.

  • ✦ Protecting ancient wild cacao genetics through agroforestry trusts.
  • ✦ Zero-carbon solar-powered conching facilities by 2027.
  • ✦ Global tasting education inspiring mindful food appreciation.
THE VISIONARIES

Meet Our Founders & Team

A harmonious partnership between an obsessive master confectioner, a passionate botanist, and a creative pastry chef.

Julian Vance

Master Chocolatier & Co-Founder

Trained in Paris and Zurich across classical patisserie and fine confectionery. Julian spent over a decade perfecting the delicate crystalline science of cacao tempering and marble enrobing before founding Meridian Cacao.

"Chocolate is not mere sugar; it is agricultural art, an intricate vintage as expressive and nuanced as grand cru wine."

Clara Thorne

Sourcing Director & Co-Founder

With a background in tropical agroforestry and botanical conservation, Clara spends four months every year living among smallholder farming cooperatives in Madagascar, Peru, and Ecuador, ensuring fair pricing and heirloom tree preservation.

Amelie Dubois

Head Pastry Chef

Amelie brings a touch of modern culinary flair to our traditional chocolate house. She constantly pushes the boundaries of flavor by incorporating seasonal, wild-foraged ingredients into our limited-edition truffles and ganaches.

THE MERIDIAN MILESTONES

Our journey

From a kitchen slab to a celebrated chocolate house. We have always stayed true to our roots: authentic ingredients, passionate craftsmanship, and an unwavering commitment to bringing you the pure, untamed flavor of real cacao.

2018 — Present

Dedicated to heirloom Criollo & Trinitario single-estate stewardship and pure bean-to-bar mastery.

2018

Julian and Clara begin micro-batch stone-grinding on a second-hand 5kg granite melanger, perfecting origin roasting temperatures.

2019

Debuted three single-estate dark bars (Madagascar 70%, Peru 75%, and Ecuador 80%), winning regional artisanal accolades.

2021

Moved into a dedicated solar-powered atelier with custom temperature-controlled marble rooms and low-velocity conching suites.

2022

Introduced hand-rolled fresh cream truffles, sea salt liquid caramels, and seasonal nut-praline confections.

2024

Launched custom drawer gift boxes with hand-tied satin ribbons and calligraphed wax-sealed greeting cards for celebratory gifting.

2026

Delighting over 50,000 discerning patrons worldwide through monthly cold-pack subscriptions, virtual masterclasses, and origin tours.
