What the calendar allows

Some of what we make exists for a few weeks and then stops, either because the fruit has gone or because the weather makes it impossible. Nothing here is restocked on request.

Feb–Mar

Sold out

Coffee harvest bar

Idukki 72% with cascara — the dried coffee cherry — steeped into the conche. Made once a year, the week the Chikmagalur pickers finish. Tastes of hibiscus and stewed plum, and confuses almost everyone.

180 bars, ₹590

Apr–Jun

On sale now

Alphonso pâte truffle

Alphonso purée cooked down to a fruit pâte, set inside a Coorg White shell. The mango is bought whole from one grower in Ratnagiri and we stop the day the crates stop arriving. No purée from a tin, ever.

Loose or in any box, ₹115 a piece

Jul–Sep

Two weeks left

Monsoon bar, 74%

Bến Tre cacao with roasted rice and a little jaggery. Ganache is miserable in monsoon humidity, so we make a bar instead — something sturdy, savoury and happy to sit in a damp cupboard.

400 bars, ₹520

Oct–Nov

Orders open 1 October

Diwali collection

A sixteen-piece box built around Indian confectionery: besan praline, saffron and pistachio, rose and black pepper, a fig-leaf ganache. Packed in a mango-wood keepsake box turned in Channapatna, which people keep for years.

Limited to 900 boxes, from ₹2,400

Dec–Jan

In development

Cask ganache, winter edition

The whisky ganache we can only make when Bengaluru finally drops below twenty degrees at night. Rested a week longer than usual, which is the whole point.

Nine-piece box, ₹1,290
