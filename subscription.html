Chocolate of the month

Whatever has just come off the press, with a card explaining the origin, the ferment and what to drink alongside it. Choose how often it arrives; skip, pause or cancel any month without writing to anyone.

How often should it arrive?

Prices shown per delivery and include tax and shipping within India. Quarterly deliveries are larger boxes, not smaller ones.

What actually arrives

The same box every subscriber gets, chosen by what is ready rather than by a schedule set in January.

Chocolate we made that month

Never old stock, never a bar that has been sitting since the last harvest. If a lot is not ready, the box ships a week late and we tell you why.

An origin card per bar

Grower, variety, harvest window, fermentation length, roast profile and the farm-gate price we paid. On the reverse, two pairings and how to serve them.

A monthly online tasting

Forty minutes with whoever made the bar, tasting along with you, on the second Sunday. Recorded if you cannot make it. Included from the Connoisseur plan up.

First refusal on limited lots

Micro-lots and seasonal boxes are offered to subscribers before they reach the shop, which is usually the only way to get them at all.

The details people ask about

Delivery dayDispatched on the 3rd, arriving within two to four working days across India.
SkippingSkip any delivery up to the 28th of the month before. No limit on how often.
PausingPause for up to six months and keep your price, even if ours goes up.
SwappingConnoisseur and Collector subscribers can swap any bar for another in the range.
GiftingThree, six or twelve deliveries, paid up front, with the first box arriving on a date you choose.
CancellingFrom your account, any time before the 28th. We will not email you to ask why.
Hot weatherMay and June shipments travel with a cold pack. If a box arrives melted, we remake it.
