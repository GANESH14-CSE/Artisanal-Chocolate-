The bean-to-bar collection

Eight bars, each from a single origin and a single harvest. Prices are for 70 g. Open a card for the variety, the harvest window, how long it fermented and exactly what is in it.

Filter by style

Dark bars carry only cacao and sugar unless the ingredient list says otherwise. Milk and white are made with our own pressed cocoa butter.

How to read a flavour profile

Nobody has added raspberry to the Kilombero bar. The fruit comes from fermentation.

The notes are real, not added

Every flavour note on our cards comes from the cacao itself — from its genetics, the soil, and above all the four to seven days it spent fermenting. Where we do add something, it appears in the ingredient list.

Percentage is not quality

A 78% bar is not better than a 66% one; it simply has less sugar and more cacao. A poorly fermented 85% will taste worse than a well-made milk bar, every time.

Eat it at room temperature

Cold chocolate tastes of almost nothing. Take it out of the fridge twenty minutes early, let a square melt on the tongue rather than chewing, and breathe out through your nose.

Not sure where to begin?

Take the Idukki 72% and the Kilombero 70% together. One is deep and spiced, the other sharp and fruity, and tasting them side by side teaches more than any description can.
