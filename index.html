THE ARTISAN JOURNEY

From Bean to Bar

True artisanal chocolate requires respect for agriculture, chemistry, and time. Discover the 5-step metamorphosis from raw cocoa fruit to handcrafted confections.

01

Cocoa Beans

Heirloom Criollo pods harvested by hand, fermented in wooden cedar boxes, and sun-dried in ocean breezes.

02

Roasting

Gentle low-temperature convection roasting that unlocks delicate terroir aromatics without scorching volatile oils.

03

Grinding

Continuous 72-hour granite stone melange refining, reducing cocoa mass particles to microscopic silky smoothness.

04

Tempering

Master chocolatiers hand-temper the molten mass on chilled Vermont marble, locking in crystalline shine and snap.

05

Handcrafted Chocolate

Individual truffles hand-dipped, gold-leaf dusted, and packaged in protective botanical foil for connoisseurs.
