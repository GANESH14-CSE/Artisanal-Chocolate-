Home · Truffles

Twelve truffles, rolled every morning

Fresh cream ganache in a shell of our own chocolate. No preservatives, no glucose syrup, a seven-day life from the day it is made. Sold loose from ₹95, or in a box you put together yourself.

The full range

Everything contains dairy. Nut and alcohol content is marked on each piece and printed again on the box.

Keeping them, briefly

Store between 16°C and 18°C, away from anything aromatic — ganache takes on the smell of a fridge within hours. If you must refrigerate, seal the box and bring it back to room temperature before opening, or condensation will bloom the shells.

Eat within seven days. We would rather you finish them in three.
